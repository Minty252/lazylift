What LazyLift does

Two drafts per message

Matching the length, tone and language of the chat. A short message gets a short reply.

It remembers people

Hobbies, plans, inside jokes: LazyLift keeps notes on every person, and the next drafts build on them.

Public Discord channels

Replies that join the running conversation, instead of "Hi, anyone here from Berlin?".

A goal per chat

You set what you want, for example to arrange a meetup. LazyLift shows what has worked and what is still open.

Translation

Messages in other languages get translated, drafts come in the language of the other person.

You send it yourself

Nothing goes out on its own. One click puts the draft into the message box, you read it and press send.

Who it is for

For you if you come across stiff in chats, think about a reply for ages, or want to meet new people and find it hard to get into a conversation. Over time you see how relaxed replies are built, and you need LazyLift less.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open a chat

    WhatsApp Web or Discord in the browser, LazyLift sits in the side panel.

  2. 2

    Click Analyze

    A few seconds later there are two drafts, plus the translation and your progress toward the goal.

  3. 3

    Send it yourself

    Insert a draft, adjust it, send. Or just take it as a nudge and find your own words.

Questions

Will the other person notice?

Nothing shows up in the chat. You send every message yourself and can change it first.

Which apps work?

WhatsApp Web and Discord in the browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge). Not the phone apps.

What happens to my chats?

Only when you click Analyze does the visible history go to Claude, Anthropic's AI model, to be read. History and notes are stored by the extension in your browser.
